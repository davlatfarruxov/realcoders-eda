Alert escalation: our EDA

Team RealCoders (3FB1C141)

How we did it

Every alert comes with a different number of transactions, so first we summarized them into one row per alert. We looked at the whole transaction history and at time windows before the alert, and split everything by direction (in/out) and type. Then we trained LightGBM and CatBoost and mixed them, using 5-fold CV. We also tested on the newest alerts separately and checked that train and test look alike.

What we noticed

  • 2,405 of 14,000 train alerts were escalated (17.2%).
  • Escalated alerts usually have more history: median 493 transactions, dismissed ones 455.
  • Last week before the alert: 20.9% of 90-day activity for escalated, 21.4% for dismissed.
  • Type mix differs the most in bank_otkazmasi transactions.
  • min_all: median -2.46 for escalated, -2.42 for dismissed.
  • q90_chiqim_bank_otkazmasi: median 1.21 for escalated, 1.43 for dismissed.
  • mean_out_minus_in_karta: median 0.441 for escalated, 0.415 for dismissed.
  • q50_chiqim_bank_otkazmasi: median 0.124 for escalated, 0.302 for dismissed.
  • q90_kirim_naqd: median 1.24 for escalated, 1.21 for dismissed.
  • Adversarial check gave AUC 0.501, so train and test look similar.

Features we made because of this

  • Counts and amounts for the last 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 90 days and for all history, to check if recent activity matters (it turned out to matter less than we expected).
  • The same numbers for each direction and type pair (8 pairs), plus the share of each type.
  • Distribution of transaction sizes: percentiles (1% to 99%), skew, share of very large or very small amounts, overall and for each pair. Some of these became our strongest features.
  • Longer windows (180 and 365 days) and monthly activity: how stable it is and whether it grows or falls.
  • Last week compared to last month or last 3 months, to catch sudden jumps.
  • Incoming vs outgoing: ratio, difference, and how many days had both.
  • Days since the last transaction, number of active days, busiest day, time between transactions.
  • Share of transactions on weekends and at night.

Summary

Recent activity turned out to be similar for both groups. What mattered more was the distribution of transaction sizes over the whole history, especially outgoing bank transfers. Our model got 0.6410 ROC-AUC in cross-validation and 0.6722 on the newest alerts.
